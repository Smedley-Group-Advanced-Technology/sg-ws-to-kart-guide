# From the Workshop App to KART

:::defaults
persona: Alex Mechanic
device: tablet

:::

Welcome. This is the official FAT Karting League guide for everyone who works on karts, runs a hub workshop or supports a hub from FKL Central or remote support.

Your hub's workshop work is moving from the **FKL Workshop App** (we call it "the Workshop App" in this guide) into **KART**, the FKL Kart Allocation & Reliability Tracking app. KART is the app the league already uses for kart allocation (deciding which karts go out at events), reliability tickets and events. From your hub's switch-over day, all workshop work happens in KART too: services, repairs, parts, campaigns, schedules and orders.

## What KART is

- **One app for the whole kart.** Status, services, repairs, reliability tickets, parts and race numbers all sit on the same kart, so everybody sees the same picture.
- **It works in a browser.** Use it on the workshop tablet, a laptop or your phone. There is nothing to install.
- **The same words you know, mostly.** Some screens have new names. The [Where did it go?](where-did-it-go.md) page maps every Workshop App screen to its place in KART.

!!! info "What changed from the Workshop App"
    - The Workshop App was a separate app. KART is the league's main app, and the workshop is one area inside it.
    - "Turn Required" is now called **Requires service**. It means exactly the same thing: the kart has been used and needs a service.
    - Incidents are information only. A stopping reliability ticket, an open damage ticket or missing batteries take a kart out of use. See [Incidents, tickets and repairs](start/problems-explained.md).

!!! note "Remember"
    No hub that uses the Workshop App has switched yet. (A few hubs never used the Workshop App and already work in KART.) Your hub lead confirms your hub's switch-over date and time at least 24 hours before. Until then, keep working the way your hub lead tells you.

## Who to ask for help

- **Your hub lead** (the person who runs your hub; in KART they usually have the Hub Manager permission): accounts, access, how your hub works, the switch-over date.
- **KART problems**, such as a kart showing the wrong status: tell your hub manager (hub lead), with the kart number. For help with KART itself, start with **AI Support** in **KART Comms**; the AI escalates to Remote Support or FKL Central. See [KART Comms](remote-support/comms.md).
- The **FKL Support** button in KART gives automatic answers. It is not a person.

The [Who to contact](switch-over/index.md#who-to-contact) table on the switch-over page has the full list.
