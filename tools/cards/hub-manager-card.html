Hub manager

Everything a mechanic does, plus. From the Workshop App to KART.

Steps

  1. Open Workshop Manager, the last Workshop tab.
  2. Kart Allocation: give karts to mechanics.
  3. Campaigns > New campaign: Integrated or Standalone, karts, parts.
  4. Work targets per person; Readiness targets per planning event.
  5. Parts needed > Create draft orders.
  6. Order List stages: Draft, Submitted, approval by the hub manager, Ordered, then Goods In.
  7. Submit drafts. The hub manager approves in Approval queue: Approve or Reject.
  8. After ordering with the supplier: Mark ordered (Approved lines only).
  9. Goods In on the Ordered line: quantity, unit cost, invoice ref., Receive stock.
  10. Hub catalogue: hide parts, preferred supplier, Request a part.
  11. Hub Manager > Pending approvals: approve sign-ups, join links, Create user.
  12. Mark a kart used by hand: Requires service on its Overview tile; Undo if wrong.
  13. Reports: the Workshop App reports in Excel, PDF or CSV.

Statuses

Ready can race
Requires service used, needs a service (was Turn Required)
In Progress service started
Paused service paused with a reason
Repair Needed (usable) open repair, kart still available
Repair Required must not be used: stopping ticket or blocking repair
Campaign Required open campaign task, kart usable
Watch outUntil your hub's stock is moved, Parts needed may list parts that are on the shelf: check the shelf before ordering.
