# FKL Central and admin: your path

:::defaults
persona: Taylor Central
device: desktop
url: /fkl-central
state: Taylor Central signed in (admin and FKL Central)
:::

FKL Central owns what every hub works from: the master parts catalogue, the service checklists per kart class and the servicing thresholds. Admins also manage user accounts and permissions.

!!! info "What changed from the Workshop App"
    - The Workshop App's **Parts Catalog**, **Catalogues**, **Catalog Requests** review and **Master Catalogue Review** are now in **FKL Central > Parts catalogue**.
    - **Checklists** are now **FKL Central > Service checklists**, one master per kart class.
    - **Manage Users** is the **Admin** page (**Admin Access** on Select your Hub).
    - The Workshop App role parts_catalog_manager is the KART permission **FKL Central**.

## In order

1. [Parts catalogue](parts-catalogue.md): master parts, catalogues and generations.
2. [Part requests](part-requests.md): review what hubs ask for.
3. [Service checklists](checklists.md): drafts and publishing per kart class.
4. [Servicing thresholds](servicing-thresholds.md): tyre, alignment, battery and axle limits.
5. [Users and permissions](users.md): accounts, permissions and deactivation (admins).

!!! note "Remember"
    Hub managers without the FKL Central permission see only a view-only **Service checklists** page.
