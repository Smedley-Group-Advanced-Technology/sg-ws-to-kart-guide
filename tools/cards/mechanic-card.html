Mechanic

Your workshop day in KART. From the Workshop App to KART.

Steps

  1. Sign in, choose your hub, tap Workshop. It opens in a new tab.
  2. Start on Overview: check the Readiness board against the karts.
  3. Open Service Queue, tap a kart under Requires service, tap Start service.
  4. On Servicing tap each cell: Unchecked, Good, Blocked. Type readings.
  5. Fitted a part? Under Components replaced tap Select, then Add to components replaced. Every part, every time.
  6. Stopping work? Pause and pick a Pause reason. Resume from Paused services.
  7. Tap Complete service, tick the declaration, Confirm.
  8. Problem on a kart: Report a kart problem. An incident is information only; after inspecting, use Raise reliability ticket or Record damage.
  9. Must not be used? Raise a stopping reliability ticket (needs Reliability). It takes the kart out of use.
  10. Repair done: Log remedy / complete repair with the parts used.
  11. Campaign tasks: Campaign work, Start work, fit parts, Complete work.
  12. Find a kart: Open kart, type the number (e.g. 207) or scan the QR.

Statuses

Ready can race
Requires service used, needs a service (was Turn Required)
In Progress service started
Paused service paused with a reason
Repair Needed (usable) open repair, kart still available
Repair Required must not be used: stopping ticket or blocking repair
Campaign Required open campaign task, kart usable
Watch outIncidents are information only and never stop a kart. Only a stopping reliability ticket takes a kart out of use. After your hub's switch time, never record anything in the Workshop App.
HelpAsk your hub lead first. KART problems: message remote support in KART Comms (bookmark the KART address ending in /comms). Give the kart number. The FKL Support button gives automatic answers only.
