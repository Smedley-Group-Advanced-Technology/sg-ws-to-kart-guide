# Frequently asked questions

!!! info "What changed from the Workshop App"
    Most answers come down to three changes: workshop work is now in **KART**, "Turn Required" is now **Requires service**, and karts are taken out of use by a **stopping reliability ticket**, an open **damage ticket** or **Missing batteries**. On the **Fleet board** every kart is green (ready), amber (needs attention) or red (blocked).

## For mechanics

**Do I need a new account?**
Yes. You need a KART account with the **Workshop** permission and access to your hub. Your hub lead arranges it before your hub's switch-over day. See [Signing in and access](start/sign-in-and-access.md).

**Can I keep using the Workshop App after the switch?**
Only to look things up. From your hub's switch time, nothing typed in the Workshop App is copied to KART. See [Switch-over day](switch-over/index.md).

**What happened to "Turn Required"?**
It is called **Requires service** in KART. Same meaning: the kart was used and needs a service.

**Why did a new tab open?**
The cards on **Select your Hub** open each area in a new browser tab. That is normal. Keep the hub selection tab open to switch areas.

**Where is the menu?**
There is no side menu. Use **Back**, the hub selection cards and the Workshop tabs. See [Finding your way](start/finding-your-way.md).

**I logged an incident. Why is the kart still available?**
Incidents are information only. If the kart must not be used, raise a stopping reliability ticket, or ask someone with the Reliability permission. See [Incidents, tickets and repairs](start/problems-explained.md).

**I work on events without the Workshop permission (for example as a contractor). Where do I log an incident?**
On the **Tracker** (the **Technical** card of your hub): tap **Log incident**. Any staff member with access to the hub can log one. It goes on the ICU board for the workshop. See [Report a problem](mechanic/report-a-problem.md#log-an-incident-from-the-tracker-events-staff-and-contractors).

**Where did Log findings go?**
Inspection findings were replaced. After inspecting a kart, use **Raise reliability ticket** or **Record damage** on the incident (kart page or ICU board), or **Resolved: no fault found**. See [Report a problem](mechanic/report-a-problem.md#follow-up-an-incident-after-inspecting-the-kart).

**I cannot choose Reliability or Damage in Report a kart problem.**
Both need the **Reliability** permission. Workshop includes it, so ask your hub manager to check your permissions; they can give it. Until then, ask a colleague who has it.

**Can I have two karts in service at once?**
No. One active service per mechanic. Starting another offers **Pause and switch**.

**My service timer ran all night. What now?**
Tell your hub manager. They can correct the times in the service report. Next time, pause with **End of Shift**.

**Why can I not complete the service?**
**Complete service** waits until every required check is answered, blocked checks are resolved, tickets on the kart are resolved and campaign tasks are done. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

**Do I have to record every part?**
Yes, in the service, the repair or the campaign task. KART takes them from stock and orders from that. It matters most in the first days after the switch-over, when stock is moved.

**How do I mark a kart used after testing?**
Ask your hub manager to tap the **Requires service** button on the kart's tile. On event days Race to Chassis does it for you.

**I put a race number on the wrong chassis.**
Drag it back, or onto the right chassis. If KART marked the wrong chassis used, tap **Undo** in the message.

**How do I find a kart fast?**
**Open kart** on Overview: type the number (for example 207) or scan its QR code.

**How do I ask for help?**
Tell your hub manager (hub lead) first, with the kart number. For help with KART itself, open **KART Comms** and start with **AI Support**; the AI escalates to Remote Support or FKL Central when a person is needed (KART Comms is KART's messenger; ask your hub lead to bookmark it on the tablet). The **FKL Support** button gives automatic answers and is not a person. You cannot start a Remote Support chat or a direct chat with a remote support person yourself: the AI escalates to them. See [Getting help](start/finding-your-way.md#getting-help).

**Who is my hub lead?**
The person who runs your hub. In KART they usually have the Hub Manager permission, so this guide also says "hub manager".

## For hub managers

**Where are Parts Needed and the order list?**
Workshop Manager > **Parts needed** and **Order List**. See [Orders](hub-manager/orders.md).

**Why does an order need approval now?**
Every line goes through **Submitted** and **Approved** before it can be marked ordered. The hub's manager or an admin approves.

**Can my purchaser approve orders?**
No. **Purchasing** can submit, mark ordered, receive and cancel, but not approve.

**How do I let a new mechanic in?**
Send them a join link, or create the account with a temporary password, in **Pending approvals**. Then make sure they have **Workshop**. See [Approvals](hub-manager/approvals.md).

**Why is a mechanic missing from Kart Allocation?**
They need the **Workshop** permission and access to your hub.

**Parts needed lists parts that are on the shelf.**
Until your hub's stock is moved from the Workshop App, the counts can be wrong. Check the shelf before ordering.

**Can I create a planning event for my readiness targets?**
Not yet. An admin creates planning events for now.

**A part is missing from Stores.**
Check whether it is hidden at your hub in **Hub catalogue**. If it is not in the catalogue, use **Request a part**. See [Hub catalogue](hub-manager/hub-catalogue.md).

**Can we change how the team works in KART?**
Changes to event or workshop staff's daily work need the approvers' sign-off and updated SOPs and training first. Talk to the KART product owner.

**When does my hub switch?**
Your hub lead confirms the date and time at least 24 hours before. No hub that uses the Workshop App has switched yet.
